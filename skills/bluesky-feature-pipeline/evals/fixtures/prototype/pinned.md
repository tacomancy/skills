# Pinned prototype — reading goals

Round 2 of stage 3, accepted and pinned under `docs/reference/prototypes/reading-goals/`, one export per prompt:

- `00-preamble.png` — palette, type, navigation, density, as the design tool settled them.
- `01-library.png` — the library surface with a **progress ring** beside the shelf list, not the one line under it the brief's decision 2 asked for; the ring carries books read over books goal.

## What the prototype changed against the brief

1. Decision 2 — a ring beside the list rather than a line under it. The tool's push-back note: a line reads as a footer and is missed; the ring was the accepted compromise.
2. Decision 1 — the ring shows books by default even when the unit is pages, because pages did not read at a glance; pages appear on hover.
3. The open question is untouched: the prototype counts every finished book of the year.

## What the prototype kept

Decisions 3 and 4 as written: past years read-only in the editor; nothing about storage is visible in the export.
